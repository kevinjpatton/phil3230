Philosophy of Video Games

About This Course

Video games are more than mere entertainment; they are complex systems of rules, narratives, and moral choices. In this course, we will investigate the unique philosophical problems posed by interactive digital media.

Can a video game be considered a work of fine art? What is the moral status of our actions within a virtual simulation? How do simulated worlds change our understanding of agency, identity, and personal responsibility? We will draw on both classical philosophical texts and contemporary media theory to answer these questions.

Key Learning Objectives:

• Analyze the aesthetic qualities unique to interactive media.
• Apply normative ethical theories to in-game decision making.
• Debate the ontological status of virtual objects and avatars.

System Requirements:

No prior gaming experience or specialized hardware is strictly required. All required playable modules are browser-based. A willingness to engage critically with digital media is essential.
